Our philosophy

Craft, composition, and discretion — a modern maison.

Velvet Scents began as a practice: a small atelier where perfumers and designers worked together to build perfumes that behave like objects — quiet, precise, and built to last. We source responsibly, favour extrait-level concentrations, and work on small, numbered batches that age before release.

Our approach blends classical French composition with contemporary restraint: top notes that promise, heart notes that reveal, and base structures that linger. Packaging is curated to reflect ritual — tactile boxes, satin ribbons, and print materials chosen for longevity.
