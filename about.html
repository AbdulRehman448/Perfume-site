The Art of Olfactory Excellence

Velvet Scents was founded with a single vision: to create perfumes that transcend time and trends. Our journey began in the vibrant markets of Dubai, where we discovered the magic of rare oud and precious resins.

Today, our atelier combines these ancient ingredients with the precision of French perfumery. Each composition is aged to perfection, ensuring a scent that is as enduring as it is evocative.
