Our Story

A Legacy of Scent & Mastery

"Fragrance is the most intense form of memory."

Born in the vibrant heart of Dubai, Velvet Scents was established with a singular vision: to create perfumes that transcend time and culture. For over 25 years, our master perfumers have blended the meticulous structure of French perfumery with the deep, mystical soul of Arabian heritage.

Every creation is an artisanal masterpiece, hand-crafted in our Dubai atelier using rare oils and precious resins sourced from the furthest corners of the earth.
